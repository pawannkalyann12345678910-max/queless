💡 Common Questions

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How does delivery and storage work? +

Your PAWMATE meals arrive frozen in 100% recyclable insulated thermal boxes. Pop the packs into your freezer. Move 2–3 packs to the fridge 24 hours prior to feeding to thaw smoothly.

How do I transition my dog from dry kibble to fresh food? +

We include a step-by-step 7-day transition guide in your starter box: Days 1–3: 25% PAWMATE / 75% old food. Days 4–6: 50% / 50%. Day 7+: 100% pure PAWMATE fresh meals.

Can I pause, adjust, or cancel anytime? +

Absolutely. You have 100% flexible control over delivery dates, portion sizing, recipe mixes, or cancellation with zero fees directly in your account dashboard.
